# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students solving extracted homework problems on an iPad with Apple Pencil, moving between saved notebook pages and exporting their work. The paper may be light or dark according to personal preference and ambient light.

## Product Purpose

Keep homework questions readable, mathematically faithful, and visually distinct from the student's own work. Presentation choices should work offline on already saved problems without another PDF extraction.

## Brand Personality

Calm, practical, personal. Use familiar controls and short, concrete labels.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded settings with repeated colour pickers, decorative panels inside panels, surprise bulk edits, or formatting controls that compete with writing.

## Design Principles

- Keep the writing surface central and unchanged by interface decoration.
- Offer coordinated colour combinations before exposing individual colour controls.
- Keep question wording and maths in the same colour family, with a distinct problem label.
- Show the scope of changes before applying them; preserve student ink, geometry, and saved content.
- Use progressive disclosure for custom colours and selected-problem lists.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support light and dark paper, readable contrast, labelled keyboard-accessible controls, generous touch targets, and reduced motion. Identify colour combinations by name and role rather than colour alone.
